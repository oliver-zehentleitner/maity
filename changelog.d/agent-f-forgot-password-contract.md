### Added

- Contract: a forgotten password is reset by mail (§2.10). `POST /auth/password-reset` always answers `202`, and the link stays valid for 30 minutes with its token in the fragment. With TOTP enrolled, the reset also needs a TOTP or recovery code: the fifth wrong code at resets of one human in 24 hours, across links, voids every open reset link and writes `second_factor_failed` (`method: password_reset`). The reset ends every session and voids an unconfirmed new address, while agent credentials stay. An address change voids open reset links. Reset mails have their own budget, `password_reset_max_per_hour` (default 30), apart from the sign-in budget the mail factor sends from. A per-human switch `email.password_reset` and an instance switch `password_reset` turn it off, and `GET /auth/methods` gains `password_reset`. Humans whose second factor is the mail get a banner pushing toward an authenticator app. The routes are not served yet.
