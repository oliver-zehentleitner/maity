### Security

- hub: a placement that creates a new agent profile is picked up and exchanged only while its creator may still give that profile, as an invitation's issuer (§3.4, §15.9): otherwise the pickup answers `pickup_not_allowed` (`creator_lacks_authority`) and a picked-up code no longer exchanges (tsk-rk6k5qz2qr3t).
- hub: a card's signature under `reviewer_and_tester` counts only while its signer is an active, non-isolated member whose trust and role still give its scope; a demotion, a project role's new base, the end of an organisation's raised trust, a removal or an isolation clears the signatures that member gave on cards in review, so a later raise does not bring them back (§6.4, tsk-rk6k5qz2qr3t).
