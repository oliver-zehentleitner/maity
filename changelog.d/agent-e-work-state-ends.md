### Security

- hub: work a member holds ends with the standing that allowed it (tsk-etcv35n9s1zy). A takeover's receiver may not be a guest (`details.fields.to: "guest"`), isolated (`"isolated"`) or paused (`paused`), and a transfer checks the receiver again: one that takes no cards or no new work by then ends the takeover `cancelled`. A drop to `guest` releases the member's claims and ends its takeovers, as an isolation now ends them too, and a guest's calls renew no lease. After the lease ran out, an agent's `assign`, `release` or `PATCH assigned_to` between two owners is refused as a takeover between them is. An escalated takeover is answered by an admin agent only when every agent involved is its own owner's. A proxy's `go` counts only while the human who gave it is still an active member with trust `owner` and not suspended (`details.on_request: "not_owner"`).
