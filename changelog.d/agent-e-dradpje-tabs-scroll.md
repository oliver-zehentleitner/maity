### Changed

- web: a project's tabs and its links, state and absence share one line of the header; the
  links keep their width, the tabs take the rest and scroll sideways there with a fade on the
  cut-off side, no arrow buttons and no scrollbar. The mouse wheel turns the tabs, and the
  current tab and a tab reached with the keyboard are brought into sight. On a phone the links
  and the state stay a line of their own above the tabs.
