### Added

- hub: no notification mail while the human is present in the web app (§18.5). `POST /me/presence` moves the session's `present_until` (new column, migration `20261111150000_present_until`) two minutes ahead, at most once a minute per session, and writes no `last_seen_at`. While `email.hold_while_online` is on (the default), the mail of an immediate item waits while a live session is present and for `online_grace_minutes` after (mail settings and `MAITY_MAIL_ONLINE_GRACE_MINUTES`, default 5), then goes if still unread. Nothing is held until the dashboard sends the call (the web part).
