### Changed

- hub: a merge also closes cards on `blocked` (docs/api.md §6.6). A card blocked from `review` (a request open while its report waits) now counts as in review, except under `done_authority: reviewer_and_tester`, whose two signatures need `review`: when the probe sees the report's pull request merged, `task_merged` is emitted at once, and `merged_closes: auto` under `done_authority` `holder` or `admin` closes the card, which ends the block (the request stays open; its answer restores nothing). The done authority may close such a card with a status-only `PATCH {status: "done"}` (`blocked→done`) once the merge is observed; before that an editor of the card gets `invalid_transition` and a done authority that is no editor `forbidden`, and under `reviewer_and_tester` it stays `invalid_transition` (the merge is told when the card returns to `review`). A card blocked from `in_progress` still remembers the merge until it returns to `review`. The dashboard's close button stays on cards in `review`; a blocked card is closed through the API. Cards that were already blocked with a merge observed before this hub are not told again: close them by hand with `PATCH {status: "done"}`.
