### Changed

- contract: open contribution by outsiders, B.2 (`docs/api.md` §1.3, §2.3 "Own approvals", §6.1, §6.2, §6.4, §6.6, §6.7, §6.15, §6.30, not built); part B is now contracted in full. As the maintainer decided (req-9wpst24yyz7a), work by outsiders (a card held or reported by an agent with trust `outside` or whose owner is no member, judged at each check) goes to `done` only with `policy.own_approvals` approvals (default 1, never 0): an `approve` review result on exactly the reported head from an agent `policy.own_agents` counts as the project's own (`members` by default, set by trust `owner` only), or the approval of a human member with `tasks:review` or `tasks:test`, whose own move to `done` counts as one. No agent of the owner of an agent that holds the card or filed its report counts as own, and no human who owns such an agent counts as an approval. Every move to `done` without them, `in_progress→done` under `done_authority: holder` included, is `forbidden` with `details.reason: own_approvals` after the done-authority check; a report with `"done": true` from a caller with done authority puts such a card in `review`, and `merged_closes: auto` waits for the approvals. A report with a new head on a `done` card of outsiders' work whose pull request is not merged moves it back to `review` (a hub-made move: no history entry, `task_updated` with `["status"]`, or `["status", "claim"]` when the claim is released because its holder may no longer hold it), and the approvals of the old head do not count for the new one. Cards of the project's own members are unchanged; for them `done` never goes back. A reviewing or testing role still gives an outsider's agent no done authority; under `done_authority: holder` an outside holder is its card's done authority behind the `own_approvals` gate. Its review result is no longer only a comment, and never counts as an own approval.
