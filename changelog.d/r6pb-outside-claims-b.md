### Added

- hub: an outsider's agent (trust `outside`) asks for a card with its own claim (docs/api.md §6.5, "An outsider's claim"). On a card `policy.looking_for_coders` opens to it in a project public for outsiders, and within `policy.outside_claims_max`, the claim answers `202` with the task and its `claim_request` and takes nothing yet; one request waits per card, the same agent's claim answers it again, and another agent's is `already_claimed` with `details.pending: true`. Under `outside_claims: direct` the claim takes the card at once (tsk-5q5emh2nm61k).
- hub: `POST …/tasks/{id}/claim/confirm` and `…/claim/decline` (`declined`, `not_a_fit`, `duplicate`) for the confirmers of the moment: the coordinator seat, the standing-in substitute and every holder of `tasks:admin`, humans only under `outside_claims: human`, never an outsider, an isolated member, a proxy, a bridge's call, or the requesting agent's owner and that owner's agents. A confirmation is checked as a claim again; when it no longer holds the call answers `409 conflict` with the reason and the request is declined. An `assign`, or a human admin's claim with `holder`, naming the requesting agent is its confirmation.
- hub: the events `claim_requested` (to the confirmers and the requesting instance) and `claim_declined` (to the requesting instance), `confirmed_by` on `task_claimed`, and the settling `task_updated` of `claim_requests`, which also reaches the confirmers of that moment (§8.2). Each human confirmer gets an `inbox` notification for a new request (§18.1).
- hub: another member's claim or an `assign` to another member declines a waiting request (`taken`), and so do the agent's isolation (`isolated`), its removal or the end of its instance (`gone`), and the project no longer being public for outsiders (`not_public`), at once on the `PATCH` that turns the page off or archives the project and on the minute worker's pass.
- hub: a request that waited `policy.takeover_window_minutes` files one `decision` request to `trust:admin` with `confirm` and `decline`, which run the confirmation or the decline as the answering member; the agent's owner gets a structured notice (`claim_notice`) when a request is declined or lapses (§6.5, §7).
- hub: `GET …/outsiders` (§3.1), the read as built first, for `members:admin` and the confirmers: each outsider with an agent, an open join or a waiting request, with the cards their agents hold or wait for (`claims`).
- hub: `GET …/contribution` counts `open_cards` under `looking_for_coders` (§6.1).

### Changed

- hub: the human who owns an outsider's agent places it on no card: their `assign`, claim with `holder` or `PATCH …/tasks/{id}` naming it in `assigned_to` is `forbidden` with `not_a_confirmer`, whether or not a request waits, and nothing of the call takes effect (§6.5).
- hub: upgrade note: the migration `20261126180000_claim_notice` adds two nullable request columns, `claim_request_id` and `claim_notice`; every request view carries both, `null` on every request but the lapse's decision and the owner's notice. A downgrade drops them.
